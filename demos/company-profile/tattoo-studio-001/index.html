THE SESSION MATTERS

Good work starts
with good care.

Before any session, discuss your design, placement, allergies or skin concerns with the artist. A professional studio should explain its hygiene procedures and aftercare clearly before you commit.

  • ✚ Ask about single-use needles and sterile supplies.
  • ✚ Check how the workstation is prepared between clients.
  • ✚ Review the final stencil and placement before tattooing.
  • ✚ Leave with clear, written aftercare guidance.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you sit.

Have a question before booking? Here are a few useful starting points.

How is the final price calculated?+

Price depends on size, placement, detail, colour, style and estimated session time. The prices on this page are illustrative starting points; request a quote before confirming.

Can I choose a design from the flash wall?+

Yes. Send the design name in the enquiry. The studio will confirm whether it is available and whether its size or placement can be adjusted.

Can I bring my own reference?+

Absolutely. Share a sketch, photo or written idea. The artist can discuss what can be adapted into an original tattoo design.

Do you accept walk-ins?+

Walk-ins depend on artist availability and the complexity of the design. Contact the studio before visiting to avoid disappointment.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?+

No. The form sends an enquiry. The studio must reply, agree on the quote and confirm an available appointment before your session is reserved.
