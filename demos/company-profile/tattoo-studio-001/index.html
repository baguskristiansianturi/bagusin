A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you book.

Good information makes for a better first conversation. If you have another question, send it to the studio.

How much does a tattoo cost?+

Pricing depends on size, detail, style and placement. Small fine line pieces start from the illustrative price shown above. We confirm a fixed quote with you before the session begins.

Do I need to book in advance?+

We recommend booking ahead, especially for larger custom pieces. Walk-ins may be possible for small designs when an artist is available, but messaging first is the best way to avoid a wait.

How does the booking deposit work?+

Once your design and price are agreed, a deposit reserves the appointment. A 30% deposit secures your agreed appointment. The remaining balance is paid at the studio.

Can I bring my own reference image?+

Absolutely. Bring a reference, sketch or a collection of ideas. Your artist will use it as a starting point and help adapt the design to your placement and preferred scale.

What should I know about aftercare?+

Your artist will provide care instructions suited to your tattoo. In general, plan to keep a fresh tattoo out of pools, the ocean and direct sun while it heals. Follow your artist's specific guidance.

Can you rework or cover an existing tattoo?+

Many existing tattoos can be reworked, but not every design is suitable for a cover-up. Share a clear photo and the approximate size so the artist can assess the options.
