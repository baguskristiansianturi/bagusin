# My Bali — Product Vision

**Status:** Exploratory product vision  
**Product owner / originator:** Bagus Kristian Sianturi  
**Portfolio:** Bagusin  
**Geographic focus:** Bali  
**Document role:** Living source of truth for the product vision

> This document records the founder's current intent and evolving vision. It is not a final business plan, legal structure, technical specification, or authorization to begin implementation.

## 1. Product ownership and portfolio

My Bali is an independent product conceived and owned by Bagus Kristian Sianturi. It belongs to the Bagusin product portfolio. It is not a BDM Trans product and is not defined as a BDM-owned platform.

BDM Trans may potentially participate as one transport or service provider in the My Bali ecosystem in the future, subject to the same provider rules as other businesses. That possibility does not confer ownership, preferential ranking, or control over My Bali.

Bagusin may also develop other independent products, including a construction and building-materials ecosystem intended to serve markets beyond Bali. Those products are separate initiatives; their details must not be mixed into My Bali's product scope.

## 2. Why My Bali should exist

The founder wants to build an application that he personally wants to use while living in and exploring Bali. Product decisions begin with genuine personal needs, frustrations, workflows, and desired experiences—not with copying competitors or chasing trends.

The founder's satisfaction comes from making something useful. Even helping one person meaningfully is a worthwhile outcome. Examples of the intended positive impact include:
- Helping an independent driver gain additional customer opportunities.
- Helping a restaurant that struggles to attract customers become discoverable.
- Helping accommodation owners reach suitable guests.
- Helping genuine activity operators, such as watersport operators, reach customers directly.
- Helping visitors plan, navigate, book, document, and remember their Bali experiences.

This founder-led approach is a starting point, not proof that every feature will work for every user. The product should remain open to learning from real use without abandoning its core purpose.

## 3. Mission — exploratory wording

To make exploring, experiencing, planning, and discovering Bali more useful and connected, while creating fair opportunities for genuine local service providers.

The mission is deliberately Bali-focused. My Bali is not currently intended as a generic template to be renamed My Bandung, My Jakarta, or another city product. Its ambition may reach users around the world, but its place-based identity and initial product focus remain Bali.

## 4. Product philosophy

1. **Founder-first utility:** Build an experience the founder genuinely wants to use.
2. **Real-world usefulness:** Prefer solving concrete problems over accumulating superficial features.
3. **Bali focus:** Develop deep local relevance rather than broad, shallow geographic coverage.
4. **Direct provider relationships:** Prioritize verified businesses and actual service operators over unauthorized intermediaries.
5. **Evidence-aware trust:** Distinguish verified identity, confirmed transactions, documented visits, and personal opinions.
6. **User agency:** Users should control what they keep private and what they publish.
7. **Continuous improvement:** My Bali is expected to evolve through ongoing use, observation, and careful revisions.
8. **Fair opportunity:** The ecosystem should create additional discovery and customer opportunities for legitimate local providers.

## 5. What My Bali is intended to become

The current concept combines several connected capabilities:
- Trip planning and itinerary assistance.
- Discovery of Bali places, businesses, services, and activities.
- Direct access to genuine providers and their availability.
- Transport and route planning, including custom journeys.
- Booking and transaction-linked activity records.
- A personal travel journal with private and public sharing controls.
- Community discovery through real travel experiences and stories.

These are concept areas, not a committed launch feature list. Their sequence, feasibility, and business model remain open.

## 6. Success and impact

The founder's first measure of success is meaningful usefulness, even to one person. Potential beneficiaries include visitors, residents, independent drivers, restaurants, accommodation owners, and direct activity operators.

Later measures may include successful bookings, repeat use, provider outcomes, user trust, itinerary usefulness, and quality of documented experiences. Metrics and numerical targets have not yet been approved.

## 7. Relationship to Bagusin

A successful My Bali product may become a significant portfolio example demonstrating Bagusin's ability to conceive, build, operate, and continuously improve a substantial digital product.

The founder's future workload may change if My Bali becomes successful. Existing Bagusin services, such as courses and other offerings, should not be assumed to stop automatically. Possible future arrangements include trained instructors, staff, documented SOPs, and delegated operations. This is a future business consideration, not a current decision to discontinue any service.

## 8. Boundaries and unresolved matters

- My Bali is not owned by BDM Trans.
- Bali remains its defined geographic focus.
- The exact legal/company structure is not established here.
- The product name, trademark, domains, and app-store availability have not been validated.
- The full feature scope, monetization, rollout, staffing, and technical architecture remain undecided.
- Do not treat exploratory examples as approved requirements.
- Do not begin coding or public launch work solely because this vision document exists.

## 9. Change discipline

This is a living document. When later discussions clarify or change an existing idea, revise the relevant section rather than appending repetitive notes. Preserve meaningful history in the repository's change records. Before changing a major product direction, summarize the proposed interpretation and obtain the founder's confirmation.
