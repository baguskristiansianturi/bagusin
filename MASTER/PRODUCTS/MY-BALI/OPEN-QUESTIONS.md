# My Bali — Open Questions and Validation Register

**Status:** Living register; questions are not decisions

This file tracks unresolved matters so exploratory conversations do not accidentally become product requirements.

## Product identity and governance

- [ ] Confirm final public product name and conduct trademark, domain, and app-store checks.
- [ ] Determine the legal/company structure for ownership and operation.
- [ ] Define how My Bali relates operationally to Bagusin while remaining a distinct product.
- [ ] Define whether and how affiliated businesses (including any future BDM Trans participation) are treated equally in ranking, verification, and enforcement.

## Provider ecosystem

- [ ] Define acceptable evidence for an owner or authorized representative.
- [ ] Decide how to handle franchises, multi-branch businesses, property managers, licensed operators, and authorized agents.
- [ ] Determine whether any intermediary model is ever permitted, and how it must be disclosed.
- [ ] Define provider category-specific onboarding requirements.
- [ ] Create provider service-quality SOPs, complaint handling, warning, suspension, removal, and appeal procedures.
- [ ] Determine how to verify permits or regulated services without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Travel records and evidence

- [ ] Decide which transaction and booking systems can create evidence-linked travel records.
- [ ] Define what counts as a completed visit or fulfilled service.
- [ ] Decide whether location/GPS evidence is needed, optional, or out of scope.
- [ ] Define privacy defaults, public/private controls, deletion, and retention.
- [ ] Decide how users can correct inaccurate records and dispute evidence labels.
- [ ] Define how stories distinguish evidence-backed facts from personal opinions.

## Itinerary and transport intelligence

- [ ] Identify reliable sources for maps, traffic, travel-time estimates, opening hours, and booking availability.
- [ ] Define how to handle stale or conflicting provider data.
- [ ] Decide whether the first itinerary builder is recommendation-based, manually editable, or both.
- [ ] Define how custom journeys and unusual routes are evaluated.
- [ ] Develop transparent transport fare-estimation rules and distinguish estimates from confirmed quotes.
- [ ] Determine booking and payment integration priorities.

## Social experience and reputation

- [ ] Define useful content types and what should not be allowed.
- [ ] Design reporting, moderation, appeals, and anti-spam mechanisms.
- [ ] Decide how sponsored content and commercial relationships are disclosed.
- [ ] Decide whether user levels exist and what evidence or behavior they reward.
- [ ] Prevent levels or public profiles from exposing private travel history.

## Business and operations

- [ ] Validate the needs of independent drivers, restaurants, accommodation owners, activity operators, and visitors.
- [ ] Determine the revenue model without undermining neutral discovery and fair provider treatment.
- [ ] Define operational staffing and support requirements.
- [ ] Define initial launch scope and a realistic validation plan.
- [ ] Define success measures beyond downloads, including actual user and provider outcomes.

## Decision discipline

For each resolved question, record:
- The decision and its status.
- The reason and supporting evidence.
- Who or what is affected.
- Any trade-offs and risks.
- The date and approval context.

Do not mark a question resolved merely because an example was discussed. The founder must confirm material product and policy decisions.
