# My Bali — Product Concept

**Status:** Exploratory concept; not a finalized specification  
**Parent portfolio:** Bagusin  
**Product owner:** Bagus Kristian Sianturi  
**Geographic focus:** Bali

## 1. Concept overview

My Bali is envisioned as a Bali-focused travel, discovery, service marketplace, and personal experience platform. It brings together the planning needs of visitors and residents with the discoverability and service information of genuine local providers.

The concept is broader than an itinerary builder, booking directory, review site, or social network alone. Those capabilities are intended to reinforce one another through a connected user journey.

## 2. Main concept areas

### 2.1 Plan a Bali trip

Users may provide arrival and departure dates, arrival time, airport or port, accommodation, interests, preferred activities, budget, and other constraints.

To reduce typing, the product may offer selectable interests and guided prompts, while allowing users to add their own notes and preferences.

### 2.2 Discover places and services

Potential discovery categories include:
- Transport and airport transfers.
- Restaurants, cafes, and food experiences.
- Accommodation, including hotels and kos/room rentals where appropriate.
- Tours and local guides.
- Activities and attractions, including watersports.
- Beach clubs and entertainment.
- Shops, boutiques, and other local businesses.

The category list is illustrative and may change.

### 2.3 Plan realistic itineraries

The system may organize saved places and activities into a day-by-day plan, considering travel time, opening hours, expected visit duration, availability, user preferences, and budget.

When a proposed sequence is impractical, the system should explain why and suggest alternatives. An unusual route should not automatically be rejected: users may choose it if the time and cost implications are clear.

### 2.4 Connect users with providers

A user should be able to discover the actual business or operator responsible for a service, inspect relevant details, and where supported check availability or make a booking.

The platform's provider verification and direct-provider principles are described in PROVIDER-ECOSYSTEM.md.

### 2.5 Keep a personal travel record

Users may maintain a private record of places visited, bookings, transactions, routes, photos, notes, and stories. They may choose to publish selected records or keep them private.

The distinction between a verified transaction, a documented visit, a user-entered location, and a personal narrative must remain clear.

### 2.6 Share useful travel experiences

Users may publish stories or trip collections that help others understand what a real journey was like. Other users may discover and adapt those experiences as inspiration, without assuming every itinerary will suit their own circumstances.

Community and content-quality safeguards are required; see TRAVEL-JOURNEY.md and OPEN-QUESTIONS.md.

## 3. Example journey: a visitor named Emma

This is an illustrative scenario, not an approved final workflow.

1. Emma discovers My Bali and enters her travel dates.
2. She records that she arrives at the airport around midday and leaves Bali several days later.
3. She identifies her accommodation and selects interests such as watersports, restaurants, shopping, and nature.
4. She saves places and activities to a wish list.
5. My Bali proposes a possible itinerary based on dates, locations, travel times, opening hours, and available booking information.
6. Emma adjusts the plan. The system highlights time conflicts, long transfers, or unavailable services and offers alternatives.
7. She books or purchases selected services where the provider supports booking through My Bali.
8. Her personal travel record can associate supported transactions or bookings with relevant activities.
9. She chooses which parts of her journey to keep private and which to publish.
10. Other users may discover her public story as inspiration.

## 4. Trust is part of the product experience

The concept depends on trustworthy provider information and honest user-generated records. My Bali should not imply that a business, transaction, visit, review, or story has been verified beyond the evidence actually available.

The product should distinguish:
- Provider identity and authority verification.
- Business/service information verification.
- Booking status.
- Payment or transaction status.
- Visit evidence.
- User-submitted content and opinions.

## 5. Intended product qualities

- Locally relevant to Bali.
- Useful for both visitors and people who return frequently or live on the island.
- Clear about who provides a service.
- Transparent about price, timing, availability, and booking status.
- Flexible enough for custom trips and unusual travel patterns.
- Respectful of user privacy and control.
- Designed to reduce misleading listings, spam, and low-quality promotional content.

## 6. Not yet decided

No final decision has been made about launch scope, app architecture, exact provider categories, booking/payment integrations, monetization, moderation staffing, ranking algorithms, or rollout stages. These must be discussed and validated before becoming requirements.
