# BagusIn — Master Identity

BagusIn is **Bagus Kristian Sianturi's internet house**.

Bagus is the person. BagusIn is the place on the internet where visitors can see what he is doing, where he is, what he has built, what he writes, what he has experienced, and what comes next.

Bagus should not be forced into a single professional costume such as software engineer, SEO specialist, web developer, agency owner, or travel blogger.

He may code, freelance, write, travel, drive, guide, do physical work, build software, or try something entirely different. The positive ambiguity is intentional.

However, freedom of identity does **not** reduce professional responsibility. Whenever Bagus accepts a project or service, the work must be handled professionally: clear brief, requirements, scope, quotation, execution, review, delivery, and appropriate aftercare.

## House model

- Home = living room / arrival
- Blog = reading room
- Work = gallery / evidence
- Services = professional service area
- Places = map / movement
- YouTube = windows into current life
- About = host's story
- Contact = direct connection
- Search = quiet place to stop and find something specific

## Editorial rule

Do not manufacture a full-looking website. The archive can be sparse on Day One. Real life, work, travel, experiments, and observations become content naturally.

## Launch

27 October 2026 is both BagusIn's launch and Bagus's 33rd birthday.

The site may quietly reveal:
- 27 October 2026
- 33 today
- A new chapter begins here.

Do not publish a long explanation of the birthday concept. Let visitors discover it naturally.

## YouTube

YouTube shows living, movement, work, travel, experiments, and everyday activity.

BagusIn provides context, writing, documentation, evidence, services, and archive.

The homepage must contain a latest-YouTube section from Day One. The thumbnail is a major visual element. The implementation must be prepared to keep displaying the newest published video.
