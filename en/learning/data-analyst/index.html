Learn Data Analyst step by step.

Turn data into findings that support better decisions. The curriculum moves from foundations to a practical project, with emphasis on understanding decisions—not just following tools.

Who it is for

Designed around your next step

Operations staff, business owners, aspiring analysts, and data-minded learners.

Starting point

No advanced background is assumed for the foundation modules. A computer and time for regular practice are recommended; hardware-specific requirements will be confirmed for relevant modules.

Learning outcomes

What you will work toward

  • Frame an analytical question
  • Clean and check data quality
  • Use spreadsheets and basic SQL
  • Present findings in a clear report
Private one-to-one learning

Before you start Data Analyst

Online lessons are available from anywhere. In-person lessons mean the instructor visits the learner's home/chosen location in Bali, subject to an agreed service area and schedule. Bagusin has no physical classroom.

Structured syllabus

Curriculum, module by module

This syllabus contains 10 meetings of 2 hours each, from foundations to a final project. Review entry skills and device requirements above.

01Business questions and KPI definitionsMeeting 1 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Turn a business question into a KPI with a definition, unit, time period, and decision it supports.

Guided practice

Choose a business scenario and write the analysis question, primary KPI, and required data.

Practice output: An analysis brief and KPI dictionary.

Review criteria: Each metric has a consistent definition and supports a real decision.

02Table structure and data typesMeeting 2 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Understand rows, columns, data types, keys, date formats, and what one row represents.

Guided practice

Inspect a sample dataset and identify column types, duplicates, and row meaning.

Practice output: A data-structure profile and initial issue list.

Review criteria: The unit of analysis is clear; similar-looking columns are not assumed equivalent.

03Data cleaning and missing valuesMeeting 3 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Identify missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and outliers that need review.

Guided practice

Define cleaning rules and compare row counts and key values before and after changes.

Practice output: A cleaned dataset and transformation notes.

Review criteria: Values are not removed without a reason; cleaning decisions are traceable.

04Formulas, lookups and validationMeeting 4 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Use formulas, lookups, cell references, and data validation correctly.

Guided practice

Build a worksheet that joins categories or calculates indicators from sample data.

Practice output: A spreadsheet with formulas and validation.

Review criteria: Formulas are tested on simple cases and unmatched values are handled.

05Pivot tables and summariesMeeting 5 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Summarise data with pivots, grouping, filters, and appropriate aggregation.

Guided practice

Compare sales or enquiries by time, category, and channel using summary tables.

Practice output: A pivot table and summary findings.

Review criteria: Totals match the source data and filters are explained.

06Visualisation and chart selectionMeeting 6 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Choose charts suited to comparison, trends, distribution, or composition.

Guided practice

Create two visualisations from the same dataset and explain which better answers the question.

Practice output: Charts with titles, labels, and context notes.

Review criteria: Visuals do not mislead with truncated scales or decorative clutter.

07Descriptive statistics and trendsMeeting 7 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Understand mean, median, variation, percentage change, and trends without confusing correlation with causation.

Guided practice

Calculate descriptive summaries and compare equivalent periods; flag insufficient data.

Practice output: Descriptive statistics and interpretation notes.

Review criteria: Methods and limitations are stated; conclusions do not exceed the evidence.

08Dashboard and data storytellingMeeting 8 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Build a dashboard with KPI hierarchy and a narrative connecting findings to action.

Guided practice

Create a one-page dashboard for one audience and write three insights with next steps.

Practice output: A simple dashboard and recommendation summary.

Review criteria: Charts are readable without lengthy explanation and each recommendation links to a finding.

09SQL basics and data sourcesMeeting 9 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Use basic SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, aggregates, and JOIN; understand data sources and quality.

Guided practice

Write queries answering simple business questions from a practice schema or available dataset.

Practice output: A set of queries and inspectable results.

Review criteria: Queries respect table grain and do not silently multiply rows through joins.

10Analysis project and recommendationsMeeting 10 · 2 hours+

Concept focus

Combine the question, cleaning, analysis, visualisation, and context-appropriate recommendations.

Guided practice

Complete a mini case study from a provided dataset and document assumptions and steps.

Practice output: An analysis project, dashboard, and written recommendations.

Review criteria: Sources and limitations are stated; recommendations are realistic and do not claim untested causation.

Practice method

From understanding to independent practice.

Learning does not stop after reading. Each stage leads towards something the learner can demonstrate and review.

01

Understand an example

Learn the concept, the reasoning behind decisions, and a practical example.

02

Guided practice

Repeat the core steps, then adapt the example to a real need.

03

Independent challenge

Solve a new problem without simply copying the provided example.

04

Review and improve

Check the result against clear criteria, document revisions, and prepare a project artefact.

Assessment: focus on completed practice, reasoning, the ability to improve the result, and a final project. Mentoring and feedback arrangements must be confirmed before enrolment; no unconfirmed certification or schedule is implied.

Practical work

Learn by building something useful.

The course should end with a concrete artifact that demonstrates the skills practised. The exact brief can be adjusted to the learner’s goal and starting level.

Suggested capstone

A cleaned dataset, SQL analysis, dashboard, and written recommendations.
