Private learning at the right level

The instructor visits you. Start at the right skill level.

Bagusin has no physical classroom. In-person sessions can be made around 50% more intensive in hands-on practice, depending on learner needs and agreed scope. Choose online learning from anywhere or a private visit to the learner's home/chosen location in Bali, subject to the agreed service area and schedule.

01

Check current skills

Recommendations depend on ability, not age. If computer basics are not comfortable yet, start with Digital Foundations—not straight into web development, cloud or AI.

02

Have the right device

Website, programming, data, cloud and design courses require a laptop/desktop for practice. A phone alone is not sufficient for these paths. Each course page lists its specific entry requirements.

03

Transparent private pricing

Check the course page for its total price, number of sessions, session length, and what is included before you enrol.

Availability, session dates, and final arrangements are confirmed before enrolment.

Choose your starting point

Learn for who you are and what you want to become capable of doing.

Business owners, employees, freelancers, students and independent learners may start at different levels. Start with a real need rather than chasing certificates alone.
