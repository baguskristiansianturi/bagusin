Build digital skills, from foundations to specialisation.

Master curriculum

16 domains · 4 levels in each domain

Every domain progresses through Foundation, Intermediate, Advanced and Professional. This is a competency map; it does not mean every class is currently open for enrolment.

01Digital FoundationsComputer administration, Office, Excel, Google Workspace and computer basics＋

Foundational and legacy topics: Computer administration, Office, Excel, Google Workspace and computer basics. Entry requirements are determined by the learner's goal, not just a software name.

Level 1

Foundation

Komputer dan sistem operasi; pengelolaan berkas; internet, email, keamanan akun; literasi digital dan produktivitas.

Level 2

Intermediate

Office dan alat kerja digital; dokumen, spreadsheet, presentasi; kolaborasi cloud; pemecahan masalah sehari-hari.

Level 3

Advanced

Workflow produktivitas; integrasi aplikasi; keamanan data; dokumentasi proses kerja.

Level 4

Professional

Merancang standar kerja digital; mengoptimalkan workflow organisasi; melatih pengguna dan mengevaluasi efisiensi.

Status: The curriculum map is planned for staged development. Class availability, instructor, schedule, format and fees must be confirmed before enrolment.
