How to use these terms

These general terms apply to BagusIn services. Read the relevant service-specific section and the final quotation or order summary before approving work. Any project-specific written agreement takes precedence to the extent permitted by applicable law. Demos illustrate design direction and do not promise that every displayed feature is included.

General · 02

General service terms

BagusIn is an independent, project-based service. Before work starts, both parties should agree in writing on the objectives, deliverables, scope, price, schedule, responsibilities, and payment terms. Clients must provide accurate information, authorised materials/access, and timely feedback. Known limitations and third-party dependencies should be explained where relevant. Requests that violate law, third-party rights, or platform policies may be declined.

Orders, corrections, and approval

Submitting an inquiry or brief does not authorise work to begin. Before confirming an order, the client must be able to review the scope, price, schedule, and payment plan. An order is agreed only when both parties approve the final details in writing. Confirmation records an agreement, not payment. Follow the invoice or quotation; do not pay before receiving consistent instructions.

Catalog prices apply only to the package and scope described. Payment milestones must be stated in the quotation or invoice before work begins. Pay only according to official instructions for the agreed order. Deliverables and changes

The scope defines what will be done, delivered, and excluded. The client is responsible for the accuracy and permitted use of supplied materials, business information, claims, prices, and contact details.

Revision rounds and feedback deadlines follow the quotation. Revisions address whether the deliverable matches the agreed brief. Cancellation requests must be made in writing. Both parties will review completed work, approved non-cancellable third-party costs, and payments received. Business performance is not guaranteed unless expressly stated. Material mismatches with the written scope can be reported for review and appropriate resolution.

Estimates depend on the scope and assumptions in the quotation. Dates may shift if materials, access, feedback, payments, or approvals are delayed, or a third-party service is disrupted. The client is responsible for rights and permissions for supplied materials. General · 11

Privacy and information

Form submissions are used to understand inquiries, respond, prepare quotations, manage orders, and handle related administration. Provide only information needed for the discussion. For questions, order corrections, payment issues, delivery discrepancies, or complaints, email baguskristian@gmail.com. Include an order reference if available, explain the issue, and avoid unnecessary sensitive data. BagusIn will review the information and discuss a reasonable next step.
